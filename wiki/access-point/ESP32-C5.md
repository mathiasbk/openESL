# ESP32-C5

**Status:** experimental (built, not tried on a board). **Labels:** 1000. **Release file:** `openesl-ap-esp32c5-n16r8-<version>.bin`

An ESP32-C5 with 16 MB flash and 8 MB PSRAM, such as the Waveshare ESP32-C5-WIFI6-KIT. About 10–15 $.

## Compared with the C6

- **More labels:** the PSRAM gives room for 1000 labels.
- **WiFi on 5 GHz** if you want it, which keeps 2.4 GHz free for the labels.
- **Same radio code** as the C6, and still one chip, so WiFi and the label radio still take turns.
- **Antenna** on the module, or external through u.FL on the WROOM-1U.
- It is newer and less common than the C6.

## Flashing

From the [web page](https://mathiasbk.github.io/openESL/#ap), the ESP32-C5 board. If you try it, please [tell us how it went](https://github.com/mathiasbk/openESL/issues).
